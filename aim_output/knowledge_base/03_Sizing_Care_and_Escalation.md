# SNITCH D2C Retailer — Sizing, Care & Escalation Policy

## 1. Sizing
- Snitch and Peter England shirts: S chest 38 in, M chest 40 in, L chest 42 in, XL chest 44 in. Tailored fit: size up if between sizes.
- Snitch, Puma, and Nike T-shirts: true to size.

## 2. Care
- Cotton shirts and Levis denim: cold machine wash at 30C, wash denim inside-out, no bleach, no tumble dry.
- Footwear and Nike shoes: wipe with a damp cloth, air dry, never machine wash.

## 3. Mandatory Escalation (the assistant must escalate)
1. Order_Status is Delivered but the customer says the parcel was not received (Proof-of-Delivery dispute).
2. Damaged, defective or wrong item on an order with Total_Amount above INR 10,000.
3. Electronics returns, third-party property damage, legal or consumer-court threats, or abusive language.
