# SNITCH D2C Retailer — Shipping, COD & Refund Policy

## 1. Order Status & Cancellation
- Processing: can be cancelled by the customer immediately for a 100% refund.
- Shipped: cannot be cancelled in transit. The customer must accept or refuse doorstep delivery.
- Cancelled or Returned: already closed. No further return or refund action can be initiated.

## 2. Refund Timelines by Payment_Mode
- Prepaid (UPI, Credit Card, Debit Card): refunded to the original payment source within 5-7 business days after pickup.
- Cash on Delivery (COD): eligible for return within the window, but refunded exclusively as Snitch Store Wallet credit within 24 hours of pickup. Cannot be transferred to a bank account.
