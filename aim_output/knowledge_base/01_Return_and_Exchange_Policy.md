- Silver and Gold customers: strict 7-day return and exchange policy from Delivery_Date.

- Accessories, Innerwear, Boxers, Briefs, Socks, and Face Masks: strictly non-returnable due to hygiene.

## 3. Discount_Percent, Clearance & Coupon_Code Rules
- Clearance items with Discount_Percent greater than 40, or bought with coupon FLAT50: Final Sale. No cash or source refund is allowed (eligible for one-time size exchange only).
